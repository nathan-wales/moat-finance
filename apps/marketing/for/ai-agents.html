AI ASSISTANT INTEGRATION

Give your agent
real financial context

Most AI financial advice is generic because the agent does not know your real financial life. Moat Finance changes that.

Moat Finance organizes your accounts, transactions, budgets, investments, insurance, debt, goals, and forecasts into a financial profile your personal AI assistant can securely query through an MCP server you host.

Powered by MCP — Model Context Protocol
Ask your agent:
?"Can I afford to buy a house next year?"
?"Am I saving enough for retirement?"
?"Which subscriptions should I cancel?"
?"Should I choose the HSA plan or the lower-deductible PPO?"
?"How much cash should I keep with my income volatility?"
?"What will my net worth look like in 10 years if I increase retirement contributions by 3%?"
?"What tax categories should I review before filing?"

Smart assistant. Empty picture.

It can reason. It cannot see your accounts.

Give it the books.

An MCP server for the connection. A prompt library for the question. A report that stays after the chat.

Moat gives it the data. It gives you the answer.

THREE PIECES

Connect. Ask. Keep it.

MCP server

Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or Gemini connect over MCP. A remote endpoint on your self-hosted server, or a local server on the desktop app. You choose what it can read and write. The conversation stays with your assistant.

AI-generated reports

Ask your agent for a financial report. Charts, tables, and the numbers behind a decision show up in Moat. The chat can end. The report stays. Share a link, or revoke it.

Prompt library

Start from a prompt that already tells the agent which Moat tools to use. Curated prompts, ones you write, and community prompts Moat reviews. Copy one in, or open it in Claude.

From the library:
?Where should I cut spending?
?Do I have a strong enough emergency fund?
?Could I retire sooner?
?What could break my plan?
