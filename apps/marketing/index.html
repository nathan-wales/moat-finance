QUICKSTART

Two ways to self-host

Docker is the easiest: one machine, one config file, three commands. Vercel + Neon is free in the cloud, with a bit more setup and your own domain. Anything that runs Node.js and Postgres works too.

  1. Option B: Vercel + Neon, free Run the web app, API, and MCP server on Vercel’s Hobby plan with a free Neon database (500 MB). Needs your own domain, about $10/year. Step-by-step guide
  2. Bring your own keys, or none Manual accounts and CSV imports work out of the box. Add your own Plaid keys when you want live bank syncing.
  3. Connect your AI assistant Point Claude, ChatGPT, or Cursor at your MCP endpoint and create a scoped access token.
  4. Want the iPhone app? Build it with your Apple Developer account and install it through TestFlight. iOS setup

RULES ENGINE

Built for people
who want control

If you self-host, you probably like things exactly the way you like them. Moat’s rules engine keeps your data clean without manual cleanup.

Create rules that automatically categorize transactions, tag expenses, flag tax-related spending, and keep your budget accurate.

The more rules you create, the smarter your financial profile becomes.

Example rules
IF merchant contains "Netflix"
THEN categorize as Subscriptions
IF merchant contains "restaurant" or "dining"
THEN tag as Business meal for tax review

THEN categorize as Salary

ABOUT MOAT FINANCE

Why I built this

Moat Finance started because I was frustrated with my budgeting app.

I wanted better rules. I wanted cleaner transactions. I wanted a system that could help me prepare for tax season instead of leaving me with a pile of uncategorized financial history at the end of the year.

Then AI assistants started getting useful.

I realized the missing piece wasn’t another chatbot. It was the financial context behind the chatbot. An agent can answer generic questions without your data. But it can’t help you make deeply personal financial decisions unless it understands your actual financial life.

The goal isn’t to replace your judgment. The goal is to give you better context.

But the more context an agent needs, the more you’re asked to trust whoever holds it. Your income, your debt, your insurance, your family’s plans — that shouldn’t live on a server you can’t inspect, run by a company that can change its pricing or shut down.

And charging almost $15/month to someone with one or two accounts always felt wrong to me.

So Moat Finance is free. Free to self-host, with all of its source code public. Run it yourself. Read every line. Fork it if you want it to work differently.
